    About

    I study how information systems are built, run, and protected, and I’m drawn to the questions that sit above the technology: what’s at risk, who owns it, and what the business should do about it.

    I’m completing a Bachelor of Science in Information Systems at Prince Sultan University, specializing in cybersecurity. My coursework has taken me through secure software development, penetration testing, and network defense, and my security operations training covers SIEM work, log analysis, and incident response.

    Alongside my studies I work on student consulting cases with the Consultancy Route for Students, and I’m part of the McKinsey.org Forward Program. Those experiences shape how I approach security: as a way to solve business problems, support digital transformation, and help organizations take risk on purpose rather than by accident.
